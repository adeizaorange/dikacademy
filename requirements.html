Students’ Requirements

These are the requirements and policy for being a student at Darul Ikhlas International Academy.

Interest and Respect

You should have a genuine interest in learning Quranic studies, Islamic teachings and the Arabic language. Please respect your tutors and classmates in every class.

Access to Necessary Equipment

You need a computer, tablet or phone with a stable internet connection. A working microphone and camera are needed for live classes.

Basic Computer Skills

You do not need to be an expert. If you can join an online class, you can start. Some help is available if you are less comfortable with technology.

Language Proficiency

Some courses need a certain level of Arabic, or of the language used in class. Tell us your level when you book a free consultation and we will place you correctly.

Commitment

Attend your classes regularly and complete your assignments. Steady practice is the best way to improve.

Study Materials

Some courses use specific textbooks or study materials. Make sure you have them, or ask us where to get them.

Respect for Cultural Sensitivities

In Islamic and Arabic courses, please respect cultural and religious values. This includes polite behaviour, dress code where it applies, and following Islamic principles.

Payment or Enrollment Fees

If your classes are not free, you should know the fees before you enrol and be ready to make the payments.

Age and Academic Level

Some courses are made for children, beginners or more advanced learners. Ask us which course fits your age and level.

Patience and Open-mindedness

Learning the Quran, Islamic studies and Arabic can be hard at times. Be patient and open to new ideas.

Access to Required Software

If a class uses a specific app or program for lessons or assignments, make sure you can use it and feel comfortable with it.

Support System

Having family or friends who support you helps a lot, especially if you are balancing classes with other commitments.

Feedback and Communication

Be open to giving and receiving feedback. Talking often with your tutors and classmates is encouraged.

Follow these requirements, stay consistent and work hard. Together they help make our online Quran, Islamic and Arabic classes a good place to learn.
